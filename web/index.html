        About this view

        Each column is one CPT, coloured by Robertson (2010) soil behaviour type (inferred from cone resistance, not samples) and drawn wider than the real 3.6 cm cone. The fence gives each CPT the stretch of line halfway to its neighbours; nothing is interpolated between them.

        Liquefaction is a screening calculation (Robertson & Wride 1998 / Robertson 2009) for a magnitude 6.2 earthquake, like 22 February 2011, at the shaking level you choose. Unit weights are estimated from the CPT, groundwater comes from the CPT, and no other corrections are applied. It isn't a design check. LPI (liquefaction potential index): 0 very low, under 5 low, 5–15 high, over 15 very high.
